# DCHS Cybersecurity

Welcome to the Dodge City High School Cybersecurity dojo.

This dojo is used for hands-on cybersecurity training. You will work through Linux, networking, system administration, security, and other cybersecurity challenges.

## Getting Started

Begin with the **Using the Dojo** module. It will introduce you to the pwn.college environment and show you how to work with the challenges.

After completing the Welcome module, begin the **Linux Luminarium** modules. Starting with **Hello Hackers**

Work carefully, read the instructions, and experiment. The goal is not just to find the flag, but to understand why the solution works.
